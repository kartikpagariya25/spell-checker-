SpellFix is on.

Type as fast as you like. Typos fix themselves on every website.

  1. Just type

    Finish a word with a space or punctuation and a clear typo is corrected on the spot: teh → the recieve → receive

  2. Want it your way? Backspace

    Press Backspace right after a fix and your original spelling comes back. SpellFix remembers and won't touch that word again.

  3. Stay in control

    Click the toolbar icon to pause SpellFix or turn it off for one site. The shortcut Alt+Shift+S toggles it anywhere.

Private by design: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere.
